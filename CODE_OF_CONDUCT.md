# Code of Conduct

Bindaas follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, patient, and respectful. Critique ideas, not people. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or imagery, doxxing, and sexualized content are not tolerated in any project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, or anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately (for example through a private GitHub security advisory on this repository, or the contact listed on the maintainers' profiles). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they consider inappropriate, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
